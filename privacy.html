Privacy Policy

Wingborn is made by Maxim Yaroshenko (Spain), who is responsible for this app. Contact: vmyaros@gmail.com.

Wingborn has no account and we do not run any server. We do not collect your name, email or contacts.

  • On your device: game progress and settings are stored on the device. If iCloud is turned on, Apple keeps a copy of the progress in your iCloud so it survives reinstalling.
  • Game Center (optional): if you are signed in, your scores are sent to Apple Game Center leaderboards.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. We only receive confirmation of what was bought, never your payment details.
  • Ads: Wingborn shows only optional reward videos that you choose to watch. They are provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Ltd.). In the EEA, UK and Switzerland we first ask your choice with Google's consent form; you can allow personalised ads or only non-personalised ones. To show and count ads, Google may process your IP address, device and app information (model, iOS version, language), ad interactions and approximate location derived from the IP address; with your consent, also identifiers for personalisation. We do not ask Apple's tracking permission, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used. Google's policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can change your choice any time: Settings → Privacy choices. After the “Remove ads” purchase you get the rewards without videos.
  • Children: the game is for all ages. Ads are limited to content suitable for families; players under the age of digital consent get only non-personalised ads.
  • Postcard: sharing a postcard uses the iOS share sheet and sends only what you choose, to whom you choose.

Your rights (GDPR): you can ask about, correct or delete data by writing to vmyaros@gmail.com. You can also complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).
