Wingborn

Wingborn is a one-hand paper-plane shooter: guide your origami plane with one finger, dodge the bullets and pick a new skill every level.

Privacy Policy

FAQ

Does Wingborn need the internet? No, the game works offline. Optional reward videos need a connection.

Are ads forced? No. Wingborn shows only optional reward videos that you choose to watch (revive, new cards, double coins). The “Remove ads” purchase gives these rewards without videos.

How do I change my ad privacy choice? Settings → Privacy choices.

How do I restore purchases? Shop → Restore purchases.
